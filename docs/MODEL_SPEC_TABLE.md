## Common to every fitted arm
- **Fit rows** (`dataset.fit_rows`): complete rows (every tabular input and every enriched feature present) of the years before the validation year, at most 2,000,000 rows, all onsets kept, negatives thinned uniformly without replacement with `numpy.random.default_rng(1)` (`models.fit.thin`). The same rows are used by every arm and both levels of a fold. The fresh test fits on the years before 2026 with the same cap and seed.
- **Inputs:** the arm's tabular columns (`arms.arm_columns`, groups from `configs/features.yaml`), plus at the enriched level the 180 weather-window columns, 17 window anomalies and 12 neighbourhood columns (209 extra columns). `boosting` and `tabular_network` read the 42 columns of `logistic_with_burn` (`ladder_fit.columns_for`).
- **Prior-shift correction after prediction** (`prior_shift.prior_shift_probs`): the fitted probability's odds are multiplied by [π_true / (1 − π_true)] / [π_fit / (1 − π_fit)], with π_true the per-state onset rate of the training years (the configured 0.0147 for the fresh states) and π_fit the fit prevalence of the arm; probabilities are clipped at 1e-15. Log loss and Brier use these corrected probabilities with the design weights; AP and the AUC views use the uncorrected scores.
- **Design weights** (`prior_shift.design_weights`, `weights.strata`): onsets weight 1; the negatives of a state-year stratum weigh (eligible cell-days of the stratum − onsets) / sampled negatives. They enter (a) the network loss, and (b) the evaluation of log loss and Brier. They do not enter the logistic or the boosting fit, which are unweighted.

## Tuning (not part of the fitted arms)
Boosting and the network were tuned once at the enriched level on the widest arm's inputs, by random search with seed 0, 12 trials each, on fits of at most 600,000 rows, rolling-origin validation years 2021-2024, selection by the log loss aligned to the validation sample's onset share (AP breaks ties); the network was tuned with one seed (seed 0). Search spaces: `configs/baseline.yaml` (`boosting_space`, `network_space`). The chosen settings are in `artifacts/results/tuned.json`; the logistic C values are fixed in `configs/models.yaml`. Every trial is in `artifacts/results/tuning_trials.csv` (24 trials, each scored on 4 folds).
