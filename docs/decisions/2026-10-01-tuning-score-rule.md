# Tuning score rule
Decision: a trial is scored on each tuning fold (validation years 2021-2024, January-July rows, same row set for every trial) by the log loss after aligning the sampling rate of the fit rows to the sampling rate of the validation rows (prior-shift with the validation onset share); AP is the tie-break. The network is tuned with one seed (seed 0 from the specification). A trial's score is the mean over the four folds.
Why: the specification says "selection by validation log loss" without defining it. The aligned loss needs no frame sizes, compares boosting and the network on one scale (the network is trained with design weights, so its fit prevalence is the weighted one), and never looks at 2025. The earlier stage of tuning (before this record) was discarded: its trials are kept outside the repository.
